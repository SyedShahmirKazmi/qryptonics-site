§ Two clocks are running

Clock one — exposure

Encrypted traffic captured today can be opened later.

Harvest-now-decrypt-later is not a forecast, it is a collection strategy already in use. Any record whose confidentiality has to survive into the 2030s — patient files, contract terms, national infrastructure telemetry, long-lived signing keys — is exposed the moment it crosses the wire, regardless of when a cryptographically relevant quantum computer actually arrives.

Most organisations cannot yet answer the first question: where is our cryptography?

Clock two — advantage

The problems you round off are worth the most.

Cargo scheduling, network capacity, portfolio construction, crew rostering, yard allocation. Classical solvers reach a good-enough answer and stop, because the search space grows faster than the hardware. Those truncations are where the margin sits, and they are precisely the shape of problem that quantum and hybrid methods are built to attack.

The formulation is the hard part. That is the work we do.

P—02

Quantum Algorithms & Optimization

Most quantum projects fail at the formulation, not the hardware. We take a real operational problem, encode it honestly, and benchmark it against the classical baseline you already run.

  • Problem encoding into QUBO and Ising form, with constraint handling and penalty tuning
  • Variational algorithm design — QAOA, VQE, and application-specific ansätze
  • Quantum annealing, gate-model and physics-inspired solvers evaluated side by side
  • Ising machines and probabilistic accelerators where they beat the gate model today
  • Circuit synthesis, transpilation and depth reduction for real device topologies
  • Error mitigation, noise characterisation and result post-processing
  • Honest benchmarking against classical and heuristic solvers, published internally

  • Hybrid pipelines where the quantum layer sits inside a classical model
  • Trainability and barren-plateau analysis before commitment
  • Quantum-assisted anomaly detection for network and infrastructure telemetry

  • Hybrid autoencoders

Hybrid & HPC Engineering

Quantum workloads do not run alone. The surrounding classical system — simulation, orchestration, data movement — decides whether any of it is usable in production.

  • Hybrid orchestration across quantum backends, clusters and cloud
  • MPI and multi-node workload distribution for variational loops
  • Classical solver acceleration where that is genuinely the right answer
  • Backend-agnostic abstraction so you are not locked to one vendor's hardware
  • Cost, latency and throughput modelling before deployment

§ How an engagement runs

  1. 01

    Assess

    Six to eight weeks. We map the cryptographic estate or the target optimisation problem, and produce a ranked, costed finding set. You own the output whether or not you continue.

  2. 02

    Design

    A migration architecture or a solution design with a defined classical baseline, success criteria agreed in advance, and an explicit statement of what we expect not to work.

  3. 03

    Build

    Delivery in production increments against the criteria set in stage two. Your engineers work alongside ours, because a capability you cannot operate is not a capability.

  4. 04

    Operate

    Monitoring, re-assessment as standards evolve, and handover. Crypto-agility means the next transition is routine — that is the point of doing it properly the first time.

§ About

Qryptonics is a quantum systems engineering company based in Doha.

We work at the point where quantum computing stops being a research topic and starts being an infrastructure decision: which algorithms your systems will still trust in a decade, and which of your operational problems are worth putting on quantum and hybrid hardware now.

The team's background spans quantum optimization and quantum machine learning research, applied cryptography, network engineering and enterprise security assurance. That combination matters here, because post-quantum migration is not a cryptography problem — it is an estate problem that happens to involve cryptography.

We are deliberately based in Qatar. The national quantum programme, the research capacity at Education City and the concentration of energy, logistics and financial infrastructure in one small market make this an unusually good place to do this work properly before exporting it.

Principles

  • Benchmark honestly. If the classical solver wins, we say so and you keep the classical solver.
  • No vendor lock. Backend-agnostic by default, on hardware and on cloud.
  • Ship to production. Pilots that cannot be operated are not deliverables.
  • Publish. Research that holds up under review is the only credential worth having in this field.
