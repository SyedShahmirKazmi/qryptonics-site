Products · PQC solutions

Post-quantum cryptography, as a managed solution.

Everything needed to move a cryptographic estate onto the NIST post-quantum standards — discovery, migration and assurance — packaged as a product engagement rather than a bespoke project. The work is the same work we describe under Services and Development, organised around a single outcome: systems that will still be trusted in ten years.

  • ML-KEM · FIPS 203
  • ML-DSA · FIPS 204
  • SLH-DSA · FIPS 205

§ What it covers

01

Discovery

A cryptographic bill of materials across code, certificates, protocols and hardware, with exposure ranked by how long each dataset must stay confidential.

02

Migration

Hybrid key establishment and staged rollout with rollback, built on crypto-agility so the next algorithm change is a configuration rather than a rebuild.

03

Assurance

QKD and QRNG integration where it is warranted, entropy validation against SP 800-90B, and the evidence pack an auditor will ask for.
